Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026
Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

Cubi is operated by WIKTOR BARON LABS, represented by Wiktor Baron, at ul. Jana Heweliusza 11/811, 80-890 Gdańsk, Poland ("Cubi," "we," "us," or "our"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

For privacy questions, requests and app support, email developer@breadlabs.app.

This policy covers the Cubi mobile app for iOS and Android.

2. The short version

  • Cubi has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number in the app.
  • Your lessons, progress, streaks and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Cubi shows no ads, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • When you buy or restore Cubi Pro, Apple or Google processes the payment and RevenueCat verifies the purchase, using an anonymous identifier.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

To give you a personal plan and keep track of your learning, Cubi saves the following on your device:

  • Your onboarding answers: whether you have solved a cube before, why you want to learn, whether you own a cube, your daily goal and when you plan to practise.
  • Your learning progress: completed lessons, XP, streaks and the days you practised, cube move counts, opened rewards and achievements.
  • Your preferences: theme, language and haptics.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not linked to your identity. It is removed when you delete the app, although your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backups) may keep a copy under their own settings.

4. Purchases

Stage 1 of Cubi is free. Cubi Pro, which unlocks every later stage, is sold as an auto-renewing yearly subscription and as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Apple and Google process all payments. We never receive your payment card or bank details. Their handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) helps us verify purchases, restore them and know whether Cubi Pro is active. When the app starts, the RevenueCat SDK creates a random, anonymous app user ID. RevenueCat then processes:

  • that anonymous ID,
  • purchase information such as the product, purchase and renewal dates, price, currency, store country and store transaction identifiers,
  • technical information needed to make requests, such as IP address, device type, operating system and app version, and language.

We do not give RevenueCat your name, email or learning progress. RevenueCat acts as our service provider. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Emails you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to reply and help you.

6. What Cubi does not do

Cubi does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It does not use advertising identifiers, show ads, or include third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

7. Why we use information (legal bases)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process purchase information to perform our contract with you (providing Cubi Pro), for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and preventing fraud, and to comply with legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules. We process support emails to answer you, based on our legitimate interest in helping our users or to take steps you ask for.

8. International transfers

RevenueCat processes information in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. How long we keep information

Information on your device is kept until you delete it or the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as they are needed to provide and verify your purchase and to meet legal obligations. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email developer@breadlabs.app. Because Cubi does not know who you are, please include details that help us find your purchase, such as the purchase date and the order number from your Apple or Google receipt.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority where you live or work.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in U.S. state privacy laws, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

11. Children

Cubi is suitable for a general audience. It has no accounts, chat or social features, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are made through Apple or Google, whose parental controls (such as Ask to Buy and Family Link) let parents approve them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Purchase information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information stored on your device is protected by your device's own security, so we recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app or on this page.
